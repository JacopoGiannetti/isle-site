How it works

One question a day.
A little more island.

01

Answer once a day

Isle asks one simple question about the habit you want to quit. Every clean day is a seed you plant on your island.

02

Animals move in

Each plant attracts different animals: the oak brings squirrels, the flowers bring rabbits. Now and then a seed blooms into a rare variant, the only way to lure rare animals.

03

Slips are part of it

If you slip, your streak starts over from zero, but your island stays. Even a meteorite crater, in time, becomes a pond where animals come to drink.

04

Storms if you disappear

Skip your answer and clouds roll in, then rain, then wind. One answer is enough to clear the sky.

05

The Path keeps going

The Path moves forward one day, always, even on bad days, and unlocks new plants and animals along the way.

06

When a craving hits

A craving only lasts a few minutes. Isle breathes with you until it passes.
